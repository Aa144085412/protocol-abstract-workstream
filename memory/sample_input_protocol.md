## Materials
- Resuspension buffer with RNase A
- Alkaline lysis buffer (NaOH/SDS)
- Neutralization buffer
- Wash buffer (ethanol added)
- Elution buffer
- Anion-exchange / silica maxiprep column

## Method
1. Grow a 100 mL overnight culture of E. coli in selective LB.
2. Pellet cells by centrifugation and discard the supernatant.
3. Resuspend the pellet in resuspension buffer until homogeneous.
4. Add lysis buffer, mix gently by inversion, and incubate at room temperature.
5. Add neutralization buffer and mix until a precipitate forms.
6. Clear the lysate by centrifugation and apply to the column.
7. Wash the column with wash buffer.
8. Elute the plasmid DNA and measure concentration and A260/A280.
